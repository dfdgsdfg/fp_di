# Runtime Lifecycle Requirements

[Spec](spec.md) · [Decisions](log.md)

## Problem Statement

A running application needs to withdraw a service without leaking its readers'
resources or restarting unrelated components. Dependency lookup alone does not
establish who releases resources, when dependent cleanup runs, or how readers
recover after a publisher returns.

`fp_di` adapts the revertible effects and reactive coeffects of *A Programming
Paradigm for Spatiotemporal Composability* to Dart. These requirements describe
the selected runtime profile, not every feature or theorem of the paper.
[Source mapping](researches/spatiotemporal-composability.researches.md) records
that boundary.

## Scope

- Typed and named keys, hierarchical visibility, and owned scopes.
- Component descriptions built from a `Dependency`, with derived shapes.
- Transactional activation, ownership of dispose functions, rollback, and dependent-first cleanup.
- Availability and publisher-identity reactivity, including optional dependencies.
- Sequential composition of reads and acquisitions inside one component (`Dependency`).
- Opt-in concurrent composition of a record of `Dependency` values inside one component (`(d1, d2).zipPar`), whose branches the author asserts to be independent.
- Scopes whose contents are declared at creation (`Scope(module: module)`, `child(module: module)`) and created by one `initialize`, each member its own node, activated by dependency with concurrency between independent members.
- Derived multi-key requirement shapes (`Dependency.require2` to `require8`, and `Dependency1` to `Dependency8`).
- Combination of a record of `Dependency` values into a `Dependency` of a record (`(d1, d2).zip`).
- A check at `initialize` that every required key can be published by the declarations of the scope and its ancestors.
- Editing a module as a value (`replacing`, `without`, `where`) and its derived shape (`publishes`, `requires`, `reads`), and the origin of each member of a composed module.
- The members of a module that require a set of keys, directly or through another member, and the module without those members (`requiring`, `withoutRequiring`), so that an application variant declares only the members that it can activate.
- Per-member nodes of an initialized scope, so a caller can read, wait for, or withdraw one member.
- A zero-input constructor (`Dependency(create:, dispose:)`) that creates its value on activation, with an optional release.
- Cost of shape derivation for deep compositions.
- Observation of a node and a scope: waiting for activation, the keys that a node waits for, and a text description of a scope.
- The cause of a lookup without a value: the error of `Scope.get` states why the key has no value and holds the failed nodes behind the key.
- The state of the runtime as an immutable value: the state machines of a node and of a scope, `Scope.state`, and listeners that the runtime calls at each transition.
- A read of the module of a scope from the scope, so that a caller compares the declarations of a scope with its state.
- Collection keys: a set key and a map key, to which components add contributions and which a reader reads as one value, with the derived collection shape of a module.
- An export of the shape of a module as versioned JSON, and as a Mermaid chart for a reader, from a library of the package that a tool script imports, without code generation.
- Cooperative cancellation of in-flight activation and memoized acquisition when disposal is requested.
- Core scope visibility and ownership consumed by adapters. The publication
  of a scope to a widget tree (`ScopeProvider`) is owned by
  [project architecture](../../architecture/requirements.md) ([R-UC-AR-UI-001](../../architecture/requirements.md#r-uc-ar-ui-001)).
- A conformance boundary usable as input to later formal modeling.

## Out of Scope

- Complete Cordis compatibility, its loader, configuration reconciliation, and HMR.
- Effect iterators, per-node inertial scheduling, general coeffect realms or interception.
- Whole-graph static validation beyond the resolvability check of `initialize` (for example unsatisfiable cycles), generated dependency typing, custom per-key equivalence.
- Automatic reversal of external emissions, arbitrary I/O, or unregistered effects.
- Proof that user-supplied dispose functions are correct or always terminate.
- Concurrent acquisition inside one component outside the branches of a `zipPar`. Other independent work overlaps only across nodes: the members of a module during `initialize`, and the nodes of a disposed scope and its descendants during `Scope.dispose`.
- A runtime check of effect independence, such as a commutativity classification of keys or declared footprints, for module members, scope teardown, or `zipPar` branches. The [lifecycle notes](../../../packages/fp_di/doc/lifecycle.md#effects-that-overlap) states the author obligation as patterns.
- A shared prefix of several `zipPar` branches, bounded concurrency, concurrent traversal of a collection, and readiness of one branch before the keys of the other branches resolve.
- Atomic visibility of the publications of several components.
- Adding a component to an initialized scope, and replacement of one member by another inside a scope. A new set of components is a new child scope.
- A lookup of every value of a type. Only a contribution to a collection key makes a member an element of that collection.
- A list kind of collection key, and decoration of a member by another member of the same key.
- Preemptive interruption of adapter code; a `CancellationToken` only requests a stop, and adapters can ignore it.
- Formal tooling and evidence policy, owned by [formal verification](../../verification/formal/spec.md); this topic does not claim a Dart refinement proof.
- Flutter widget behavior, including inactive-child gating; see [project architecture](../../architecture/spec.md#flutter-integration).

## User Stories

- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-001"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-001** — As an application author, I want a change of publisher, by withdrawal or by a nearer scope, to release only the affected dependents, so that unrelated features keep running.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-003"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-003** — As an application author, I want logout during the initialization of a session scope to stop cooperative pending work promptly and the next login to start only after the previous session's cleanup, so that switching accounts is fast, exposes no partial session, and cannot let old cleanup erase new state.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-004"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-004** — As an application author, I want a package to export a bundle of independent components that I compose with other bundles, so that one member that waits for a dependency does not block its siblings and a failed initialization leaves no node.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-005"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-005** — As an application author, I want to withdraw one member of an initialized scope, so that a test or a feature flag removes one node without disposing the whole scope.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-006"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-006** — As an application author, I want a registration list to hold a component whose value is built only on activation, so that a service dropped by a profile filter is never constructed.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-007"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-007** — As an application author, I want the slow members of a module to activate at the same time, so that application startup takes as long as the slowest chain of members and not the sum of all members, and a member still becomes visible only when it is ready.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-009"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-009** — As a test author, I want to replace one member of a module with a fake before a scope exists, and to see which keys a module needs from its ancestors, so that a test declares its scope once and a missing ancestor key is visible before activation.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-018"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-018** — As an application author, I want to remove from a module each member that requires a key that a variant does not publish, directly or through another member, and to read the removed members as a module, so that each scope of a variant declares a module that `initialize` accepts, and a test compares the removed members with an expected list.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-008"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-008** — As an application author, I want to wait until a component activates and to see what a component waits for, so that bootstrap code and debugging do not depend on the activation order.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-017"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-017** — As an application author, I want the error of a lookup without a value to hold the activation failures behind the key, so that an error view and a log show the cause without a listener on `Scope.errors`.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-014"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-014** — As an adapter author, I want to read the state of a scope and of its nodes as one immutable value and to be called at each transition, so that a view compares two values and shows no stale state.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-015"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-015** — As a tool author, I want to read the module of a scope from the scope, so that a tool compares the declarations of a scope with its state and keeps no second reference to the module.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-016"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-016** — As a tool author, I want the state of each scope to hold the module of that scope, so that a tool that holds only the root reads the declarations and the state of every scope.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-002"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-002** — As a component author, I want acquisition and release to share an owner, so that initialization failure and removal do not strand created values.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-011"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-011** — As an application author, I want to export the shape of each application variant as data, so that a review shows how the wiring changes and a tool finds the chains of members that `initialize` must activate one after another.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-012"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-012** — As an application author, I want the releases of unrelated members to overlap when a scope is disposed, so that logout takes as long as the slowest chain of releases and not their sum.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-010"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-010** — As an application author, I want several feature packages to contribute elements of one interface to one collection, so that the reader of the collection does not list its elements, and a platform or test variant adds, removes, or replaces an element without a change to the reader.
- <a id="r-us-rt-lc-013"></a>**R-US-RT-LC-013** — As a component author, I want to create several independent owned values of one component at the same time, so that the component activates in the time of its slowest branch and still publishes the values together as one record.

## Use Cases

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-001"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-001** — Activate a declared component
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: initialization of its scope or a relevant publisher change
  - Preconditions: a live scope
  - Main flow: the runtime MUST wait until all external `requires` keys resolve, run setup, and publish staging only after successful setup and validation.
  - Alternative flows: a duplicate publication or an unreachable key MUST fail activation and roll back completed acquisitions.
  - Postcondition: the node is active with an owned result; or dormant without published bindings while requirements are unmet or after a reported automatic activation failure; or, when an activation started by `initialize` fails, disposed with `initialize` reporting the failure, as R-UC-RT-LC-009 states.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-002"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-002** — Release owned work
  - Actor: owner
  - Trigger: setup failure, member disposal, or scope disposal
  - Preconditions: effects yielded dispose functions
  - Main flow: the runtime MUST retain each callback until its release attempt and invoke callbacks in reverse composition order: a callback runs only after the callbacks of every effect that its effect precedes. Effects that the composition leaves unordered, such as the branches of a `zipPar`, MAY release at the same time (R-UC-RT-LC-022).
  - Alternative flows: release failure MUST NOT skip remaining callbacks and MUST be reported.
  - Postcondition: each registered lease has exactly one release attempt per acquisition.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-003"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-003** — Withdraw a publisher
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: disposal or changed target
  - Preconditions: active readers depend on that publisher
  - Main flow: the publisher MUST stop accepting new readers; transitive dependents MUST finish cleanup before its own release.
  - Alternative flows: cleanup failures MUST be aggregated; only a node's own cleanup failure blocks its automatic reacquisition.
  - Postcondition: the values that a reader read stay alive throughout its releases. Permanently disposed nodes MUST NOT reactivate.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-004"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-004** — Reconcile dependencies
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: initialization of a scope, withdrawal of a member or a scope, shadowing by a nearer scope that initializes later, or activation
  - Preconditions: finite stable structure, terminating callbacks, and no obstructing cycle in the exercised graph
  - Main flow: changed publisher identities of read keys MUST cause deactivation and eligible reactivation.
  - Alternative flows: absent optional keys MUST NOT block activation; failed activation MUST NOT be retried indefinitely within one reconciliation. A reconciliation MUST repeat while its own activations change the target of an active node, and MUST stop after one pass more than the number of nodes with a `DependencyCycleError` for optional feedback that does not settle. That error MUST fail an `initialize` call whose own member is still stale, and MUST otherwise be reported through lifecycle errors.
  - Postcondition: at successful quiescence, active committed targets MUST match resolved targets; unchanged targets MUST NOT cause reexecution.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-005"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-005** — Compose acquisitions
  - Actor: component author
  - Trigger: activation of a component whose `Dependency` chains reads and `map` steps, with or without `dispose`
  - Preconditions: none
  - Main flow: the steps of one component MUST run in composition order, one after another; releases MUST run in reverse composition order.
  - Alternative flows: a failed step MUST roll back the completed acquisitions in reverse order, and the component MUST publish no binding.
  - Postcondition: publication and release order are deterministic. The steps of one component MUST NOT overlap, except the branches of one `zipPar` (R-UC-RT-LC-022).

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-007"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-007** — Create a memoized value on first use
  - Actor: component author
  - Trigger: the first `Lazy.force()` on a holder created by `Dependency.lazy`
  - Preconditions: an active owner whose keys were read during activation
  - Main flow: concurrent first calls MUST share one acquisition, and the owner MUST release it during its cleanup only if it was acquired.
  - Alternative flows: a failed acquisition MUST be reported to the caller, MUST NOT be cached, and MUST NOT deactivate the owner; `force()` after release MUST fail without acquiring.
  - Postcondition: an acquisition in flight at release is awaited and released once before the values that the same component created earlier. The keys of the `Dependency` are read at activation, so a change of their publishers reactivates the owner, which releases the old holder.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-008"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-008** — Cancel work whose owner is being disposed
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: disposal requested for a member node, its scope, or an ancestor scope; withdrawal requested for a publisher read by in-flight work; a local failure; or deactivation of an active episode
  - Preconditions: an activation episode, possibly with in-flight acquisitions or memoized acquisitions
  - Main flow: the `CancellationToken` of the episode MUST be canceled when disposal is requested, before the disposal reaches the mutation queue; an activation whose owner disposal was requested MUST NOT commit and MUST roll back once its acquisitions settle.
  - Alternative flows: an activation not yet started when disposal was requested MUST NOT run setup; the interpreter MUST start no further composition step or primitive in a failure region or its descendants after the token of that region is canceled, so an adapter that ignores the token delays only its own settlement and keeps its lease owned. Cancelable effects and resources MUST receive the episode token; cancellation MUST NOT change component shapes or abort dispose functions. Withdrawal requests MUST cancel in-flight dependents transitively, and such dependents MUST become dormant rather than removed, and they MUST NOT become eligible until that withdrawal finishes. The episode root and each `Lazy` creation are failure regions; a `Lazy` creation is a child of its owner's root. A local failure MUST cancel the token of its region before the first dispose function of that region. The failure MUST NOT cancel work outside the region unless the failure propagates there. The token state MUST change at the trigger; listener execution order relative to cleanup is not guaranteed. Outside the runtime, `CancellationToken.none` MUST never be canceled, and a `CancellationController` MUST cancel its token once, on its first `cancel()`.
  - Postcondition: no binding of the canceled activation is published; every returned lease has one release attempt; failure caused by a disposal request is reported as `ActivationCanceledException`, distinct from `ActivationException`. A mutation requested after a disposal MUST NOT begin before that disposal's rollback and cleanup attempts finish; cancellation shortens the canceled prefix and never reorders it with later work.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-006"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-006** — Own a scoped environment
  - Actor: application or adapter
  - Trigger: scope creation or disposal
  - Preconditions: a live parent
  - Main flow: a scope MUST take its declared module at creation and MUST create nothing before `initialize`. Local bindings MUST take precedence over parent bindings; siblings MUST remain isolated. A child MAY initialize before its parent; its nodes then wait for the keys that the parent publishes later.
  - Alternative flows: disposing a scope MUST permanently dispose its nodes and its descendants, initialized or not.
  - Postcondition: scope disposal releases descendants and leaves its parent usable. The publication of a scope to a widget tree and its replacement follow [R-UC-AR-UI-001](../../architecture/requirements.md#r-uc-ar-ui-001).

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-009"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-009** — Initialize a scope as independent nodes
  - Actor: application author
  - Trigger: `Scope.initialize` on a scope declared with `Scope(module: module)` or `child(module: module)`
  - Preconditions: a live scope that is not initialized; terminating member callbacks
  - Main flow: the runtime MUST create every declared member as its own node, in declaration order, inside one mutation. It MUST activate the members as R-UC-RT-LC-014 states and MUST reconcile once at the end. A member whose requirements are unmet MUST start dormant and MUST activate during that reconciliation.
  - Alternative flows: if the activation of any member throws, the outcome MUST NOT depend on member order. The failure MAY occur during the activation of the members or when the member first activates during the closing reconciliation. The runtime MUST deactivate and dispose every node that the call created, in reverse declaration order, and MUST then rethrow. The scope MUST then stay uninitialized, and a later `initialize` MAY retry unless a disposal of the scope or an ancestor was requested. R-UC-RT-LC-014 states the rule for members that are still activating. A node of a descendant scope that wakes because the scope publishes its key follows R-UC-RT-LC-004: its failure is reported through lifecycle errors and `initialize` succeeds. Cleanup failures during that rollback MUST be reported with the activation failure. Dormancy MUST NOT count as failure. Before it creates any node, `initialize` MUST throw `DuplicateKeyError` when two members of the scope publish the same key. The error MUST name the key and the labels of those members. A mutation requested from inside a running mutation of the same runtime MUST fail with `ReentrantMutationError` before it changes any state. A callback that a finished mutation created, such as a stream listener, MAY request a mutation, which then queues. `initialize` on an initialized scope MUST throw a `StateError`, and on a disposed scope MUST throw `DisposedScopeError`. Before it creates any node, `initialize` MUST throw `UndeclaredKeyError` when a member requires a key that no other member of the scope and no member declared by an ancestor publishes, initialized or not. The error MUST name each such member and key. Optional reads MUST NOT be checked. When optional reads among the members feed back and do not settle within the bound of R-UC-RT-LC-004, `initialize` MUST fail with `DependencyCycleError` and roll back.
  - Postcondition: after success, `initialize` completes with the scope, `isInitialized` is true, and the members have nodes that activate or wait independently. After failure, no node of the scope stays in the runtime, and a node of a descendant scope that depended on its bindings is dormant. Disposing the scope MUST deactivate its nodes permanently inside one mutation as R-UC-RT-LC-021 states, reconcile once, and aggregate dispose failures. A second disposal MUST do nothing.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-014"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-014** — Activate module members by dependency, concurrently
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: `Scope.initialize`, for a module built with `Module(components: components)`, `Module.all`, or `+`
  - Preconditions: a live scope; derived member shapes
  - Main flow: a member MUST depend on a member of the same module when that member publishes a key that the first member reads. The keys MUST be the only order between members: a member that needs the effect of another requires a token key that the other publishes. A member MUST start only after every member it depends on has settled. A member settles when its activation returns, committed or dormant. A member with no such dependency MUST start at once, so independent members activate at the same time. A member MUST publish its bindings when its own activation succeeds and not later. A publisher MUST commit before a dependent starts. The commit order of independent members follows completion order and is not deterministic. A listener MUST observe the commits in commit order.
  - Alternative flows: members that publish the same key never start, because `initialize` rejects them first (R-UC-RT-LC-009). If the dependencies of members form a cycle, the runtime MUST start the first unstarted member in declaration order when no member is in flight or ready. If the activation of any member throws, the runtime MUST start no further member and MUST wait for every member that is in flight to settle. It MUST then deactivate and dispose every node that the call created, in reverse declaration order, and MUST rethrow the failure of the first failed member in declaration order, with rollback failures attached as in R-UC-RT-LC-009. The runtime MUST NOT cancel a member in flight.
  - Postcondition: the rollback of a failed call stays in reverse declaration order, with the dependents of each node deactivated before the node as in R-UC-RT-LC-003. Scope disposal releases the members as R-UC-RT-LC-021 states. Per-member nodes and the closing reconciliation keep their contracts. A node of another scope keeps the rules of R-UC-RT-LC-004.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-010"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-010** — Require several keys as one derived shape
  - Actor: component author
  - Trigger: `Dependency.require2` to `Dependency.require8`, with `mapN`, or `Dependency1` to `Dependency8`
  - Preconditions: none
  - Main flow: the shape MUST be derived: `requires` is the given keys, `optional` is empty, and `key` is null. The component MUST stay dormant until every key resolves, and then MUST yield the values as a record in key order.
  - Alternative flows: a key MAY be named through `key1` to `key8`. `mapN` MUST keep the source shape, with or without `dispose`. `DependencyN` MUST have the shape of the matching `requireN` and MUST pass the values in key order to `create`. `create` and `dispose` MUST behave as the matching `mapN` with the same `create` and `dispose`. A `DependencyN` MUST be a `Dependency` of its value type, so it chains.
  - Postcondition: a missing key keeps the component dormant, and `Dependency.require` remains the one-key form.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-015"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-015** — Combine a record of `Dependency` values
  - Actor: component author
  - Trigger: `zip` on a record of 2 to 8 `Dependency` values
  - Preconditions: none
  - Main flow: the result MUST yield the values as a record in record order. The shape MUST be the union of the shapes of the `Dependency` values. The `Dependency` values MUST run left to right, one after another.
  - Alternative flows: a record of `Dependency.require` members MUST have the shape of the `requireN` with the same keys. A missing required key MUST keep the component dormant.
  - Postcondition: the resources of the `Dependency` values are released right to left.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-022"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-022** — Combine a record of `Dependency` values concurrently
  - Actor: component author
  - Trigger: activation of a component whose `Dependency` contains `zipPar` on a record of 2 to 8 `Dependency` values
  - Preconditions: the effects of different branches are independent, as the [independence contract](spec.md#independence-contract) states: every key at which two branches operate has a commutative interface, or the author shows independence directly.
  - Main flow: the runtime MUST start every branch before it awaits any branch, unless the token of the enclosing failure region is canceled before that branch starts. A branch that does not start MUST run no step, own nothing, and yield no value. Each branch MUST run its own steps in order. The result MUST yield the values as a record in record order, whatever order the branches complete in. The shape MUST equal the shape of `zip` on the same record. The release of the group MUST start the releases of all branches at the same time. Each branch MUST release in reverse order of its own steps. The steps after the group MUST release before the group, and the steps before the group MUST release after it.
  - Alternative flows: when a branch fails, the runtime MUST cancel the token of the enclosing failure region at once, so the other branches stop at their next checkpoint. The group MUST wait until every branch settles before the first dispose function of that region runs. Every acquisition of every branch MUST have one release attempt, also an acquisition that returns after the cancellation. The reported cause MUST be the first genuine failure in record order. A cancellation that the token caused is not a genuine failure. The exception MUST list the other genuine failures in `branchFailures`, in record order. A genuine failure MUST be reported as `ActivationException`, although it canceled the token. When owner disposal or a withdrawal canceled the token first, the outcome MUST stay `ActivationCanceledException`.
  - Postcondition: the component publishes nothing unless every branch and every later step succeed. The dispose failures of the group follow reverse record order, whatever order the releases finish in.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-023"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-023** — Bound the cost of the cycle check of a read
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: an activation reads a value that a committed publisher holds
  - Preconditions: a scope whose committed reads form no cycle
  - Main flow: the runtime MUST reject a read that closes a cycle of reads with `DependencyCycleError`. When no node reads a value of the reading node, the check SHOULD cost constant time, so `initialize` of a module costs time near-linear in its members plus its reads.
  - Alternative flows: when another node reads a value of the reading node, the check MAY walk the committed reads.
  - Postcondition: the runtime reports the same cycles as a check that walks the committed reads on every read.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-024"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-024** — Check internal invariants in debug builds
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: a runtime state change that a self-check covers
  - Preconditions: Dart assertions are enabled, as in `package:test`, `dart run --enable-asserts`, and a Flutter debug build
  - Main flow: the runtime MUST evaluate each self-check only inside an `assert`. A failed self-check MUST throw an `AssertionError` that names its invariant.
  - Alternative flows: when assertions are disabled, the runtime MUST NOT evaluate a self-check. A self-check MUST NOT change state, so the public results and errors are the same with and without assertions. The runtime MUST read a test-only fault, deliberate defect, or self-check mute only inside an `assert`, so a build without assertions behaves as if none is set.
  - Postcondition: a failed self-check always shows a runtime defect, never a misuse by the caller. Errors for misuse stay public errors in every build.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-025"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-025** — Name a key of a core value type
  - Actor: component author
  - Trigger: the construction of a `Component` whose key, or a key that its `Dependency` reads, is a `DiKey`
  - Preconditions: Dart erases an extension type to its representation type at run time, so `DiKey<PoolSize>()` of an extension type `PoolSize` over `int` equals `DiKey<int>()`.
  - Main flow: the `Component` constructor MUST throw an `ArgumentError` when its key or a key that its `Dependency` reads is a `DiKey` without a name whose type is a [core value type](spec.md#key-names). The error MUST name the key and show a key with `name` and an extension type with its own named key.
  - Alternative flows: a named `DiKey`, a collection key, a map entry, and a `DiKey` without a name of any other type pass the check.
  - Postcondition: an extension type over a core value type cannot share the key of its representation type or of another extension type without a deliberate shared name. The check runs in every build, because it rejects a misuse (R-UC-RT-LC-024).

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-026"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-026** — Publish the key of the type argument by default
  - Actor: component author
  - Trigger: the construction of a `Component`
  - Preconditions: none
  - Main flow: `Component<A>(dependency:)` and `Component<A>.value(value:)` without `key` MUST publish `DiKey<A>()`. With `key`, they MUST publish that key, whose type MUST be exactly `A`. `Component.effect(dependency:)` MUST publish nothing; its value is not visible to another member.
  - Alternative flows: `Component` and `Component.value` MUST throw an `ArgumentError` that names `Component.effect` when `A` is `void`, `dynamic`, or `Null`. A default key of a core value type fails as R-UC-RT-LC-025 states.
  - Postcondition: every component publishes exactly one key, except an effect and a set contribution, whose constructors state that they publish none.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-012"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-012** — Dispose one member of an initialized scope
  - Actor: application author
  - Trigger: disposal of a `Scope.node` or `Scope.nodes` node, or scope disposal after a member disposal
  - Preconditions: an initialized scope
  - Main flow: `Scope.nodes` MUST list one `ComponentNode` for each declared component in declaration order, and MUST be empty before `initialize`. `Scope.node(key:)` MUST return the node of the member that publishes the key, typed as the key; it MUST throw a `StateError` before `initialize` and an `ArgumentError` when no member publishes the key. A member node MUST read the member value, report whether the member is active, and dispose the member alone. Disposing one member MUST withdraw its bindings, MUST deactivate its dependents first, and MUST reconcile once.
  - Alternative flows: scope disposal MUST skip a member that is already disposed. It MUST NOT deactivate that member again and MUST NOT fail because of it. A second disposal of a member MUST NOT release anything again and MUST complete with the result of the disposal in progress. - <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-016"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-016** — Observe a node and a scope
  - Actor: application author or adapter
  - Trigger: a read of `ComponentNode.whenActive`, `ComponentNode.missing`, `ComponentNode.state`, `ComponentNode.activationException`, `Scope.findNode`, `Scope.isDisposed`, or `Scope.describe`, or a mutation that runs longer than the `slowMutationThreshold` of a root created with `onSlowMutation`

Each node entry MUST state the label, the state, the published keys, and the missing keys. The state MUST distinguish a node whose activation runs from a node whose last activation failed. The state MUST be `deactivating` from the withdrawal of the keys of a committed activation until the release of that activation ends. `ComponentNode.isActive` MUST be true only in the state `active`, and the node MUST return its value only in that state. A member of a running `initialize` whose activation failed, or whose activation a disposal request canceled, MUST keep the state `activating` until the rollback of that call disposes it. The `phase` of `ComponentNode.state` MUST be the state that `describe` states for the node, and `disposed` for a disposed node. `ComponentNode.activationException` MUST return the failure of the last activation while the state is `activationFailed`. The cause of that failure MUST be the error that the activation threw, with the stack trace of that error. `activationException` MUST be null in every other state. `Scope.findNode` MUST return the node that published the value that `get` returns for the key. When `get` has no value, `findNode` MUST return the nearest node whose component publishes the key, from the scope to the root, and null when no such node exists. A scope without nodes and a member that was disposed alone MUST NOT count. `Scope.isDisposed` MUST be true from a `dispose` call that the runtime accepts for the scope or an ancestor. `isDisposed` MUST NOT become false again, and the read MUST NOT throw. The label MUST be the `debugLabel` when one is set and otherwise the published key. A scope that is not initialized MUST list each declared component that has no node with the state `declared`. A component MUST NOT have more than one entry. A read MUST NOT start a mutation or change the state of the runtime.
  - Alternative flows: when the node is disposed before it activates, `whenActive` MUST complete with an `InactiveNodeException` error. A failed automatic activation MUST NOT complete `whenActive`. The failure goes to `Scope.errors`, and a later successful activation completes `whenActive`.
  - Alternative flows: a root with `onSlowMutation` MUST report a mutation that runs longer than its threshold, and again after each further threshold while it runs, with the operation, the elapsed time, the activations in flight, those whose token is canceled, the owners whose dispose functions run, and the number of queued mutations. A listener MAY request a mutation, which MUST queue rather than fail as reentrant. A root without `onSlowMutation` MUST NOT start a timer.
  - Postcondition: the state is unchanged. The text of `describe` is for people and is not a stable format.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-030"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-030** — State the cause of a lookup without a value
  - Actor: application author or adapter
  - Trigger: a `Scope.get` call for a key that has no value
  - Preconditions: a scope whose lookups answer, in a phase before `disposing`
  - Main flow: `Scope.get` MUST throw an `UnavailableKeyException` that holds the key. `failedNodeStates` of the exception MUST hold the `NodeState` of each failed node behind the key, each one time. A search MUST find those nodes. The search MUST start at the node that `Scope.findNode` returns for the key. A node in the phase `activationFailed` is a failed node, and the search MUST NOT continue behind it. For a node in the phase `waiting`, the search MUST continue at the nodes of each key in `missing`, in the order of `missing`. The node of a `DiKey` MUST be the nearest node whose component publishes the key, from the scope of the waiting node to the root. The waiting node itself MUST NOT count. The nodes of a collection key MUST be the contributing nodes of that collection for the waiting node whose contribution does not resolve, in the order of the collection. The search MUST NOT continue behind a node in another phase. `failedNodeStates` MUST be in the order in which a depth-first search reaches the failed nodes. The message MUST state which case holds: no node publishes the key, a scope in the phase `declared` declares the key, the node waits, the activation of the node failed, or the node has another phase. For a waiting node, the message MUST list the `missing` keys. When `failedNodeStates` is not empty, the message MUST state the label and the cause of the first failed node and the number of the other failed nodes. `Scope.maybeGet` MUST return null in each case.
  - Alternative flows: when no node publishes the key, or no failed node is behind the key, `failedNodeStates` MUST be empty. An `UnavailableKeyException` that a read inside an activation throws MUST have empty `failedNodeStates`, and its message MUST state only that the key has no value.
  - Postcondition: the call starts no mutation and changes no state. The search visits each node at most one time. A `get` call that returns a value MUST NOT run the search.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-028"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-028** — Hold the state of the runtime as an immutable value
  - Actor: application author or adapter
  - Trigger: a read of `Scope.state`, or a transition of the runtime while a listener of `Scope.addListener` is added
  - Preconditions: a root scope
  - Main flow: the state of the runtime is the state of each scope of one root and of each node of those scopes. A scope MUST be in one phase: `declared`, `initializing`, `initialized`, `disposeRequested`, `disposing`, or `disposed`. The phase MUST be `initializing` from the start of the `initialize` mutation, which creates the nodes, until that call completes or the runtime accepts a `dispose` call for the scope or an ancestor. After a failed `initialize`, the phase MUST be `declared` again, without nodes, only when the runtime accepted no such `dispose` call before the rollback of the `initialize` ended. Otherwise the phase MUST stay `disposeRequested` at the end of that rollback, and the `ScopeState` MUST keep the `NodeState` of each node of the call, with the phase `disposed`. The phase MUST be `disposeRequested` from a `dispose` call that the runtime accepts for the scope or an ancestor, `disposing` from the start of the disposal mutation, and `disposed` when the disposal of the scope ends. The phase of a scope MUST NOT return from `disposeRequested`, `disposing`, or `disposed` to an earlier phase, so `isDisposed` MUST NOT return to false. A node MUST be in one phase: `waiting`, `inactive`, `activating`, `active`, `deactivating`, `activationFailed`, `disposeFailed`, or `disposed`. `Scope.state` MUST return a `ScopeState`. A `ScopeState` MUST hold the phase of the scope, the module of the scope, a `NodeState` for each node of the scope in declaration order, and the `ScopeState` of each child scope that is not disposed. A `NodeState` MUST hold the number of the node, which no other node of the root has, the label, the published key, the phase, the `missing` keys, the `activationException`, and the number of commits of the node. A `ScopeState` and a `NodeState` MUST NOT change after the runtime returns them. Two `NodeState` values with equal members MUST be equal, and two `ScopeState` values with an equal phase, the same module, equal nodes, and equal children MUST be equal. Two `ScopeState` values with different modules MUST NOT be equal. `ComponentNode.state` MUST equal the `NodeState` of that node in the `ScopeState` of its scope, and `ComponentNode.missing` and `ComponentNode.activationException` MUST equal its members. `isDisposed` MUST be true only in the phases `disposeRequested`, `disposing`, and `disposed`. A lookup of a scope MUST throw `DisposedScopeError` only in the phases `disposing` and `disposed`. In another phase, `get` MUST return a value for a key only while the node that `findNode` returns for that key has the phase `active`, and that value MUST be the value of that node. A transition MUST replace the state of the runtime once, MUST increase `revision` by one, and MUST then call each listener of the root once. The transitions MUST be: the creation of a child scope, the creation of the nodes of a scope, the start of an activation, a commit, the end of an activation that did not commit and that leaves the phase `activating`, the start of a release, the end of a release, the disposal of a node that is not active, and each change of the phase of a scope. A commit MUST make the keys of the node resolve and the phase `active` in one transition, and it MUST increase the number of commits by one. The start of a release MUST withdraw the keys and make the phase `deactivating` in one transition. A transition MUST keep the `NodeState` object of each node that it does not change, and the `ScopeState` object of each scope whose value it does not change. Two reads of `Scope.state` without a transition between them MUST return the same object. A read of `Scope.state` MUST NOT throw in any phase and MUST NOT start a mutation. A listener is a function without an argument. A read of `Scope.state` inside a listener MUST return the current state. The current state is the state after the transition of that call, unless a listener started a later transition. `ScopeState.changedSince` MUST return, for an earlier value of the same scope, a pair of the earlier and the later `NodeState` for each node of the scope and its descendants whose two values are not equal. A created node MUST have no earlier value in its pair, and a node that the later value does not hold MUST have no later value.
  - Alternative flows: a transition of a publisher can change the phase and the `missing` keys of a node that waits for its key. The state after that transition MUST show each such change. The runtime MUST complete the listener calls of one transition before the calls of the next. When a listener starts a transition, that transition MUST replace the state at once, and its listener calls MUST follow the calls of the transition in progress. The runtime MUST report a failure to `Scope.errors` after the listener calls of the transition that shows the failure. A subscriber of `Scope.errors` can start a transition. The runtime MUST report a failure of that transition after the report in progress.
  - Alternative flows: `addListener` MUST throw `DisposedScopeError` while `isDisposed` is true, and `removeListener` MUST NOT throw. After the transition that makes a scope `disposed`, the runtime MUST call the listeners of that scope and then remove them. After the disposal of a scope, the runtime MUST NOT hold a node or a key of that scope for the state. A function that is added to two scopes MUST be a listener of each scope, and `removeListener` on one scope MUST NOT remove the function from the other scope. A second `addListener` of one function on one scope MUST NOT add a second listener. A listener that throws MUST be removed, `Scope.errors` MUST report a `DiListenerException`, and the other listeners MUST still be called.
  - Alternative flows: a mutation that a listener requests inside a mutation MUST fail with `ReentrantMutationError`. The creation of a child scope and an accepted `dispose` call are transitions that can run outside a mutation. An accepted `dispose` call MUST record its disposal before it calls the listeners. A `dispose` call for that scope from such a listener MUST complete with that disposal, and a mutation that the listener requests MUST run after that disposal.
  - Alternative flows: `isInitialized` is true from the completion of an `initialize` call with success and stays true afterward, so the phase alone does not give it. An accepted `dispose` call that cancels no activation of a member of a running `initialize` MUST NOT fail that call: the call MUST complete with success. `isInitialized` is then true, and the phase MUST stay `disposeRequested`: the scope does not have the phase `initialized`. `Scope.nodes` is empty while `isInitialized` is false, while the `ScopeState` holds the nodes from `initializing`. The module of a scope does not change, so the state copies no shape and refers to the module. The `NodeState` at a position belongs to the component at that position of the module. The runtime has no change event. A read of `Scope.state` after a transition SHOULD cost time linear in the nodes of the scopes that the transition changed.
  - Postcondition: a caller that holds two `ScopeState` values compares them without a read of the runtime.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-029"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-029** — Read the module of a scope
  - Actor: application author or tool author
  - Trigger: a read of `Scope.module`, or of `module` of a `ScopeState`
  - Preconditions: a scope, in any phase; for a `ScopeState`, a value that `Scope.state` or `childStates` returned
  - Main flow: `Scope.module` MUST return the `Module` that the scope was created with. Each read MUST return the same object. A scope that was created without a module MUST return an empty module. The read MUST answer in each phase of the scope, also in `disposing` and `disposed`, and MUST NOT throw. The read MUST NOT start a mutation and MUST NOT change `revision`. The component at a position of `Scope.module.components` MUST be the component of the `NodeState` at that position of `Scope.state.nodeStates`. `module` of a `ScopeState` MUST be the object that `Scope.module` returns for the scope of that value. The component at a position of its `components` MUST be the component of the `NodeState` at that position of the `nodeStates` of the same value.
  - Alternative flows: the module holds the declarations of that scope only. It MUST NOT hold a component of an ancestor or of a child scope. `publishes`, `requires`, and `reads` of the module state declared keys. They do not state the keys that resolve now: `NodeState.missing` and `Scope.findNode` state those. An edit of the module returns a new module and MUST NOT change the scope. A `ScopeState` holds no ancestor, so no read of a value reaches the module of an ancestor. A value that a caller keeps after the disposal of its scope still returns the module.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-017"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-017** — Edit a module as a value
  - Actor: application or test author
  - Trigger: `Module.replacing`, `Module.without`, `Module.where`, `Module.overriddenBy`, or a read of `publishes`, `requires`, `reads`, or `origins`
  - Preconditions: none
  - Main flow: each edit MUST return a new module and MUST NOT change its source. `replacing(key, component)` MUST put the component at the position of the one member that publishes the key. `without(key)` MUST remove the one member that publishes the key. `where(test)` MUST keep the members for which the test returns true, in declaration order. `overriddenBy(overrides)` MUST put each member of `overrides` at the position of the one member that publishes the same key and MUST keep every other member. `publishes` MUST be the union of the published keys of the members. `requires` MUST be the required keys that no other member publishes. `reads` MUST be the required and optional keys that no other member publishes and the collection keys that members read, so it contains `requires`. `origins` MUST state the origin of each member: the label of the module whose constructor listed the member, or no label. A concatenation with `Module.all` or `+` MUST keep the origin of each member. A label of `Module.all` MUST be the label of the result and MUST NOT change an origin. An edit MUST keep the origin of each position, so a member that `replacing` or `overriddenBy` puts in has the origin of the member that it replaces.
  - Alternative flows: `replacing` and `without` MUST throw an `ArgumentError` when no member or more than one member publishes the key. `replacing` MUST throw an `ArgumentError` when the component does not publish the key. `overriddenBy` MUST throw an `ArgumentError` when a member of `overrides` publishes no key, such as an effect or a set contribution, when no member or more than one member publishes its key, or when two members of `overrides` publish the same key.
  - Postcondition: the derived sets and `origins` are unmodifiable. A member that requires a key it publishes itself still counts that key in `requires`. No decision of the runtime reads an origin.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-031"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-031** — Select the members that require absent keys
  - Actor: application or test author
  - Trigger: `Module.requiring(keys:, ancestorKeys:)` or `Module.withoutRequiring(keys:, ancestorKeys:)`
  - Preconditions: none
  - Main flow: each key of `keys` is an absent key. `requiring` MUST return a module of each member that requires an absent key. A member requires a key when `requires` of the member holds the key. The key that a member of the result publishes MUST also be an absent key, unless `ancestorKeys` holds that key. The result MUST be the smallest set of members that satisfies these rules. `withoutRequiring` MUST return a module of each member that the result of `requiring` for the same arguments does not hold. Each of the two results MUST keep the declaration order, the origin of each member, and the `debugLabel` of the source, as `where` does. Each call MUST return a new module and MUST NOT change its source. A call without `ancestorKeys` MUST treat no key as a key of an ancestor.
  - Alternative flows: an optional read and a collection read MUST NOT put a member in the result of `requiring`, because the resolvability check of `initialize` does not read them. A strict reader of a collection key therefore stays when a contributing member is in the result. A member that requires the key of a map entry in the result MUST be in the result, because an entry key is a published key. A member that publishes a key of `keys` MUST NOT be in the result for that reason, and each member that requires that key MUST be in the result. `ancestorKeys` MUST NOT remove a key from `keys`. When two members publish one key and one of them is in the result, that key MUST be an absent key, unless `ancestorKeys` holds it. A member that requires the key that it publishes reads that key from an ancestor, so a caller that removes such a member lists the key in `ancestorKeys` to keep its other readers. A key that no member requires and a collection key in `keys` or in `ancestorKeys` MUST NOT change a result. The two calls MUST NOT throw.
  - Postcondition: no member of the result of `withoutRequiring` requires a key of `keys`. Each key in `requires` of that result is in `requires` of the source or in `ancestorKeys`. A call SHOULD cost time near-linear in the members plus their required keys.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-018"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-018** — Contribute to and read a collection key
  - Actor: application author, runtime
  - Trigger: `initialize` of a scope whose members contribute to a collection key or read one, an activation or deactivation of a contributor, the disposal of a contributor or of a scope, and the initialization of a scope that contributes
  - Preconditions: a set key or a map key of element type `T` and a name, declared once and shared by its contributors and readers
  - Main flow (identity): the identity of a collection key MUST be its kind, `T`, and its name. The identity of an entry key MUST be its map key and its `String` id. An entry key MUST NOT equal a plain `DiKey`, which has no map key, and MUST NOT equal an entry key of another collection, also for the same `T` and the same id. A collection key MUST NOT equal a `DiKey` of its value type.
  - Main flow (contribution): a set contribution MUST add the value of its component to the set key and MUST NOT publish another key. A map entry MUST be the publication of an entry key. An entry key MUST be a key that `Component(key:)` accepts and that `Dependency.require` reads, so another member, including another entry, MUST be able to depend on one entry with the existing key reads. Each contributor MUST remain its own node with its own dormancy and reactivation.
  - Main flow (membership): the contributions to a collection for a reader MUST be those of the contributors that are declared in the scope of the reader or in an ancestor and that are not disposed. A parent MUST NOT see the contributors of a child. A contributor that is disposed by itself MUST leave the membership. A contributor that deactivates and can activate again MUST keep its contribution in the membership. The disposal of a scope disposes every reader that can see the contributors of that scope, so it MUST NOT change a remaining reader. The own contribution of a reader MUST NOT be in the membership of its own read, because a component never reads its own publication.
  - Main flow (selection): each contribution to a set key MUST be selected. For each id of a map key, the selected entry MUST be the entry that `Dependency.require` of that entry key resolves from the scope of the reader: the nearest active entry. An id MUST be unresolved when no entry of that id is active.
  - Main flow (reads): a strict collection read MUST yield the selected values when every contribution to a set key is active, or every id of a map key is resolved. Otherwise the reader MUST wait, and an active reader MUST deactivate, as for a missing required key. An optional collection read MUST yield the values of the active set contributions, or of the resolved ids, and MUST NOT wait. A set read MUST yield a `Set<T>`, so equal values of two contributors MUST yield one element. A map read MUST yield a `Map<String, T>`.
  - Main flow (order): the elements MUST follow declaration order, the contributions of ancestors before those of a nearer scope. An element that two contributors yield MUST keep the position of the first. An id MUST keep the position of its first declaration, also when an entry of a nearer scope is selected.
  - Main flow (scheduling): a reader MUST start only after every other contributor of the same scope settles. A collection with no contribution MUST yield an empty value, and a strict read of it MUST activate at once.
  - Alternative flows: two entries of one map key with the same id in one scope MUST make `initialize` throw `DuplicateKeyError` before any member is created. A collection read MUST NOT make `initialize` throw `UndeclaredKeyError`, because an empty collection is valid. A change of the membership or of a selected identity MUST change the target of every reader that can see the collection, and the reader MUST activate again with the new value when its read is satisfied.
  - Postcondition: a reader deactivates before each contributor that it selected, so its release can use the values that it read. A member that implements `T` and does not contribute is not an element. The runtime does not detect a contribution to a wrong collection key; the collection is then smaller. An application declares each collection key once as a constant that contributors import, and a test compares `contributes` of every variant.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-019"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-019** — Derive the collection shape of a module
  - Actor: application or test author
  - Trigger: a read of the shape of a component or a module that contributes to or reads a collection key, or an edit of such a module
  - Preconditions: none
  - Main flow: the shape of a component MUST keep its strict and its optional collection reads apart. Entry keys MUST count in `publishes`, as other published keys do. `contributes` MUST list the set keys to which members contribute, each with its number of contributors. A strict or an optional collection read MUST count in `reads` and MUST NOT count in `requires`. `overriddenBy` MUST replace a map entry by its entry key, as it replaces another published key.
  - Alternative flows: a set contribution and an effect publish no key, so `replacing`, `without`, and `overriddenBy` MUST NOT select them; `where` selects them.
  - Postcondition: the derived sets are unmodifiable. A test can compare the contributions of every variant from module values, without a runtime.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-020"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-020** — Export the shape of a module
  - Actor: application or test author, through a tool script
  - Trigger: `ShapeGraph.of(module, ancestorModules:)` with `toJson()`, `toMermaid()`, or `toMermaid(collapsed: true)`
  - Preconditions: a module value and, optionally, the modules of its ancestor scopes, root first
  - Main flow: the export MUST NOT create a node or run a `create`. It MUST list each member in declaration order with its label, its origin, its published key, its set key, and its required, optional, strict collection, and optional collection reads. It MUST list an edge from a member to each other member of the module that the member waits for in `initialize`: a publisher of a key that it reads, a contributor to a set key that it reads, and a publisher of an entry of a map key that it reads, with the kind of the read. The depth of a member MUST be one more than the largest depth of the members that it waits for, so members of one depth can activate at the same time. The export MUST list one longest chain of such members. The JSON MUST state its format and a version, and the keys MUST appear as their text. The Mermaid text MUST draw each member as one node with its label and each edge as one line whose form states the kind of the read. The Mermaid text MUST draw the members of one origin as one boundary with that label, inside the boundary of their scope; a member without an origin, or with the label of its module, is outside such a boundary. With ancestors, the Mermaid text MUST draw each scope as one boundary. A plain read MUST go to the nearest scope that declares a publisher, and a collection read to its members in every ancestor. The collapsed Mermaid text MUST draw one node for each origin of a scope, and one node for the members of the scope outside such a boundary, each with the number of its members.   - Alternative flows: the export MUST list the required keys that neither the module nor an ancestor publishes, and the keys that two members publish, without throwing, so a test can report every problem of a variant.   - Postcondition: the module is unchanged. A field added to the JSON keeps the version; a changed or removed field changes it. The Mermaid text is a view for a reader: it has no version, and a tool reads the JSON. The export states structure, not durations.


- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-021"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-021** — Release a disposed scope along its reads
  - Actor: runtime
  - Trigger: `Scope.dispose`
  - Preconditions: the nodes of the scope and its descendants, with the values that each active node read when it committed
  - Main flow: a node MUST start its release only after every node that read one of its values has finished its own release, so each release runs while the values it read are alive. The committed reads MUST be the only order between releases: creation order MUST NOT delay a release. Nodes that do not wait for each other MUST release at the same time. The dispose functions of one node MUST run in reverse composition order: along a chain of steps one after another, last registered first, and the branches of a `zipPar` at the same time (R-UC-RT-LC-022).
  - Alternative flows: when reads form a cycle and no node is ready or releasing, the runtime MUST start the unstarted node that was created last; it releases its live dependents first, depth first. The dispose failures of the `DisposeException` MUST follow reverse creation order, whatever order the releases finished in; the dispose failures of a cycle fallback follow its depth-first release order at the place of the node that started it.
  - Postcondition: `dispose` completes after every release has settled. The rollback of a failed `initialize`, a disposal of one member, and a withdrawal keep their sequential order (R-UC-RT-LC-009, R-UC-RT-LC-012, R-UC-RT-LC-003). Releases that overlap need independent effects, as members that activate at the same time do ([independence contract](spec.md#independence-contract)); a token key orders two members whose effects are not independent.

- <a id="r-uc-rt-lc-027"></a>**R-UC-RT-LC-027** — Call the runtime from a zone other than the zone of an earlier call
  - Actor: application author or test author
  - Trigger: a call of `initialize`, `dispose`, `ComponentNode.dispose`, `ComponentNode.whenActive`, `Lazy.force`, or `CancellationToken.whenCanceled`, after an earlier call from another zone has finished
  - Preconditions: the zone of the earlier call MAY have stopped. A stopped zone never runs the microtasks that it holds. The body of a Flutter `testWidgets` test is such a zone after it completes.
  - Main flow: a later call MUST NOT depend on the zone of an earlier, finished call. The runtime MUST NOT add a listener to a completed future that an earlier call created. The runtime MUST NOT wait for a microtask that a finished call left in its zone. A mutation MUST still start after one asynchronous hop in the zone of its caller, never inside the call.
  - Alternative flows: an operation that is still in flight when its own zone stops cannot finish, because its microtasks never run. The runtime MUST NOT try to repair that case, and MUST NOT schedule work on the root zone. A `whenActive` or `whenCanceled` listener that a stopped zone registered never runs. Callers in one zone MAY share a waiter; callers in different zones MUST NOT. The order of mutations, the detection of reentrant calls, cancellation, and the delivery of errors keep their rules (R-UC-RT-LC-008, R-UC-RT-LC-009).
  - Postcondition: a mutation requested after a finished mutation starts and completes, whatever happened to the zone of the finished one. Mutations still run one at a time in the order of their calls.
